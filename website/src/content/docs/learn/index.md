This course teaches you iMBrace: understand it, see it work, build on it and build it well,
in English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese. Pick the path that fits you and read
the pages in order.

## Pick your path

**Explain iMBrace to a customer.**
[What iMBrace is](/learn/understand/overview/), then
[What iMBrace does today](/learn/understand/capability-truth-table/), then
[A tour of the platform](/learn/see-it-work/platform-tour/), then
[CV screening, end to end](/learn/see-it-work/cv-screening/) or
[Sales quoting](/learn/see-it-work/sales-quoting/).

**Build on iMBrace.**
[Getting started](/learn/build-it/getting-started/), then
[The building blocks](/learn/build-it/building-blocks/), then
[the tutorial](/learn/build-it/vibe-coding-tutorial/), then
[Working with an AI coding tool](/learn/build-it/working-with-ai/), then
[Build it well](/learn/build-it-well/). The hands-on tutorial takes about an hour.

**Build with an AI coding tool.**
Start with the [Build guide for AI tools](/learn/build-guide/). Your tool can also read
[the whole course as one file](https://engineer.imbrace.co/learn/llms-full.txt).

**Work with iMBrace as a partner.**
The partner kit adds demo scripts, delivery and deployment guides and a practice
organisation. Ask your iMBrace contact for it.
